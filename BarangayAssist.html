What it does

Everything a resident asks the barangay for, in one app

Each service has a matching screen in the staff dashboard, so nothing a resident submits gets lost between the window and the logbook.

Request documents

Residents pick the document, see the requirements, and submit. They get a reference number and a notice when it is ready for pickup.

Sample ref: BA-2024-0128

Book appointments

Pickups and visits get a date and time slot. Staff confirm from the dashboard, and the day's schedule is visible at a glance.

Pickup: 9:00 AM, Confirmed

Report concerns

Potholes, broken streetlights and uncollected garbage are reported with a photo and the purok. Staff assign each one and update its status.

New, In Progress, Resolved

Post announcements

Clean-up drives, flag raising schedules and vaccination drives are posted once and appear on every resident's home screen. Drafts stay hidden until published.

Draft or Published

Know your residents

A searchable list by purok with contact numbers, request history and verification status, so staff can confirm who is asking.

Verified or Pending Verification

See the whole picture

The dashboard totals requests, pending items, resolved reports and today's appointments, so the captain can see the workload without opening a logbook.

Totals update as staff act
How it works

From a phone tap to a signed document

  1. Resident submits

    Chooses a document or describes a concern, then sends it from the app.

  2. Staff review

    The request appears in the dashboard marked Pending, with the resident's details.

  3. Staff approve

    One tap approves a request, confirms an appointment, or assigns a concern.

  4. Resident is notified

    The status changes in the app and the resident knows when to pick up or what was fixed.

For barangay staff

One dashboard instead of a stack of logbooks

Pending items are marked in amber so they are the first thing staff see each morning.
